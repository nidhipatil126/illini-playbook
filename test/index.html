    How the Fuel tab sets your meal goals and picks foods. Ballpark only.

      Free time of 20+ minutes gets a label:

        3. How the 3 foods are picked

        Each food gets a score for how far it is from your meal goal:

        score = |food protein − goal protein| ÷ goal protein + |food carbs − goal carbs| ÷ goal carbs

        The 3 lowest scores are shown. Dividing by the goal makes protein and carbs count equally. Foods that change when your goal changes are marked New.

        Not counted: fat, calories, sex, body type, goals like cutting, and allergies. Every meal gets the same goal, and menus are samples.
